## 3. Why integrated

**Section heading:** Most security gaps live between vendors.

Threats do not respect the line between the physical and the digital. A tailgater slips through a door while the camera watching it records to nobody. An alarm trips at 2am and sits unanswered. A single phishing email opens the door your guards were never asked to watch. When your guarding company, your camera installer and your IT security are three separate suppliers, the space between them becomes your weakest point, and no one owns the full picture.

Supreme Warrior was built to close that space. We cover all three layers, physical, electronic and cyber, as one team with one point of accountability. Your guards, your cameras and your digital defences work to the same plan, reviewed together, answerable to the same people. Fewer suppliers to manage, fewer blind spots to exploit.

Professional guarding built around your site and its risks, not a body at a desk. Our officers are screened, licensed and trained for the environment they work in, whether that is a condominium lobby, a mall concourse, a bank branch or a construction perimeter.

### Electronic Security

The eyes and locks that never sleep. We design, install and maintain surveillance and access systems, then back them with monitoring so a recorded incident becomes a prevented one. Everything is specified for your building and integrated with your guarding, so alarms reach a person who can act.

*What this covers:* CCTV installation, remote video monitoring, alarm systems and monitoring, access control, intercom and video entry, perimeter intrusion detection, fire alarm and detection, and number-plate recognition.

**Link:** Explore electronic security

### Cybersecurity

The threats you cannot see on a camera. We test your systems the way an attacker would, find the weaknesses before they are used against you, and stand watch over your network so an intrusion is caught early and contained. Essential for any business holding customer data, taking payments, or running operations that cannot afford downtime.

*What this covers:* penetration testing, vulnerability assessment, managed detection and response, network security, security audits and compliance, phishing simulation and awareness, endpoint security, and incident response.
